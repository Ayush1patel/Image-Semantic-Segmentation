Takeaways:
- Every class appears in at least 230 val images (Solar_Board, the
  rarest) — none are absent, so none get NaN'd out of the mIoU mean.
- Green_Field (39%) + Wild_Field (32%) dominate pixel count (~71%
  combined) — these large, visually simple regions pull the macro-average
  up, but the per-class table in `classwise_val_iou_results.md` shows the
  mean isn't hiding poor rare-class performance: it's a straight,
  unweighted mean over all 18 classes regardless of their pixel share.
